FPR base scores range from 0.0 (skip it) to 10.0 (order immediately) and are derived from a weighted average across seven metrics.

Marking Taste as "haven't tried it" removes Taste and Fast from scoring and reweights the remaining five metrics.

This rating is a decision-support heuristic only and is not a substitute for professional nutritional or financial advice.
